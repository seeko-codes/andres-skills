# Theoretical basis and limits

Read only the source needed to resolve a specific question; this is not startup context.

- [Parnas, 1972](https://www.cs.lafayette.edu/~gexia/cs301/resources/parnas.html): decomposition by hidden design decisions, distinguished from processing sequence.
- [Rao & Ballard, 1999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10195184/): hierarchical predictions and correction through residual error.
- [Friston et al., 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25689102/): epistemic and extrinsic value in action selection.
- [Smith, Friston & Whyte, 2022](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10143770/): practical active-inference formulation and the distinction between model and environment.
- [Millidge, Tschantz & Buckley, 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.08128): why expected free energy is not simply variational free energy projected into the future; exploration depends on the formulation. The probabilistic complexity term is not code size or context usage.

Active inference motivates informative action as well as goal-directed action. Our resource
costs, human authority, and acceptance gates are explicit engineering choices. Informal
action scores are not free-energy measurements. A formal implementation would need a
specified probabilistic model of states, observations, actions, and preferences plus tested
inference/planning machinery; these Markdown instructions do not supply that machinery.

The construction order and backbone vocabulary draw on the ICS corpus's layers of learning and importance-based chunking. Their application to agent-built repositories is a proposed transfer, not an ICS-authored software method.
