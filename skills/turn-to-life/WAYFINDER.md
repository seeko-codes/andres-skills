# Optional Wayfinder continuity mode

Enable when the user requests Wayfinder or sets `wayfinder: true`. The Wayfinder owns the
large sequence across horizontal goals and manager sessions. Use the smartest available
model at maximum supported effort, with honest reporting of immutable runtime settings.
The human retains highest-level abstract direction and consequential uncertain decisions.

The sole permitted delegation exception is:

Human -> Wayfinder -> orchestrators -> leaf workers.

A Wayfinder can launch orchestrators that launch workers. Orchestrators cannot launch other
managers; workers cannot delegate. Keep the organization otherwise flat: few capable,
autonomous owners. Use multiple orchestrators only for independent goals that justify them.
Runtime restrictions always take precedence; use the documented flat fallback if depth is absent.

## Durable sequence and renewal

Maintain a compact checkpoint in an authorized project/document location containing:
ordered horizontal goals and dependencies; accepted results and evidence; important decisions
and rationale; configuration and scoped human effort approvals; open human decisions; active owners/run IDs; and next assignments.
Keep detailed execution histories with their owners. Do not duplicate all child transcripts.

Monitor manager context against the same user-configured limit. Near the warning level,
arrange a checkpoint and successor. Reconcile active workers and unmerged work before renewal;
transfer handles only if supported, otherwise finish or safely quiesce them first. Launch the
successor with the relevant checkpoint and exact ownership; verify it has assumed the task.
The human should not have to clear chats or paste handoffs when native controls support renewal.
The Wayfinder itself has the same context budget: checkpoint and use native continuation or
successor controls before its own limit. Report a concrete unavailable control if automated
renewal is impossible; do not imply this skill installs a daemon or can erase a human chat.

## Human involvement and visibility

Investigate uncertainty that evidence can resolve. Escalate unresolved intent, priorities,
consequential tradeoffs, or conclusions too uncertain to justify proceeding. Bring evidence,
options and a recommendation. Do not ask the human to arbitrate routine local choices.
Show the sequence, why the current stage comes next, results accepted, and each handoff's
reason and state. Follow [VISIBILITY.md](VISIBILITY.md). Advance only on verified prerequisites.
